# Corrective tranche specification

The candidate pool contains exactly 100 Turkish single-turn SFT records: 20
per repair family. Each record has one dominant teaching objective and a
concise proposed target. Human review must check correctness, natural Turkish,
necessity of the final sentence, unsupported causality, repetition, and
whether a clarifying question is genuinely required.

Answers should normally be one to three sentences. Clarification examples ask
one high-value question before domain-specific advice. Precision examples
balance distractor order and query direction; positional shortcuts are not
allowed. Emotional examples avoid both fabricated feelings and categorical
claims that exceed available evidence. Consistency examples must preserve a
single position from first sentence to last.

The 100-record tranche remains separately identifiable when appended to the
858-row exp-005 artifact. No final-holdout prompt, paraphrase, answer, or source
scenario may enter this pool.
