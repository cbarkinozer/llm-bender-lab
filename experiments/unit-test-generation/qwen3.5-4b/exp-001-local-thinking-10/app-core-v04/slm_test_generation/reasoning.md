A passing pytest exit status alone is insufficient: skipped, uncalled and untaken assertions provide no reached oracle. Exact equality is strong even when its expected value is wrong, and execution failure must disable success gates. The fixture runner derives its gate from actual pytest outcomes. One successful observation among four eligible tasks is 25%, even with two observations missing. One killed mutant out of four eligible and two valid tasks gives 25% unconditional and 50% conditional; empty denominators are unavailable.
