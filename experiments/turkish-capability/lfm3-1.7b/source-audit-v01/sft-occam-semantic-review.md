# SFT and Occam source semantic review

Agent review, 2026-11-14. `human_reviewed: false` throughout. Read repository AGENTS.md, dataset guide (including qualitative versus statistical review, provenance, teacher bias and leakage), and local source cards. This is a bounded, purposive qualitative audit, not an error-rate estimate, human certification, source approval or training authorization. No source rows were changed; no paid requests were made.

Pins: Turkish-SFT-v1.0 `5a5a852ab9067866d72ed92381205518feaae5fb`; Occam `947850d484c71f657dbec2bb7e3d356cb5fb23e3`. Local metadata matches these pins, and reviewed raw training files match download-manifest SHA256. Every reviewed original was matched to its one-based raw source line.

Occam source boundary: all reviewed Occam data come solely from the pinned public HF cbarkinozer/Occam-Turkish-Response-SFT release. No historical local versions or drafts were consulted or added, and none are required as an additional source. Public metadata provenance gaps remain documented limitations.

Scope: 25 complete SFT records selected from the 36 parent samples, including short/fragment targets and long technical, scientific, cultural, planning and interpersonal responses; all 38 Occam training records in the 42 parent samples. The four sampled Occam validation records were excluded from semantic training review. Occam validation is development data and never a training candidate. Parent inventory: SFT 5,579; Occam 124 = 104 train + 20 validation. No denominator-based defect rates are inferred.

SFT's system explicitly requests academic depth, Turkish context, examples and interdisciplinary analysis. Long answers are therefore not automatically instruction failures. The main risks are false precision, unsupported authority, artificial historical/cultural attachments and broken targets. Lines 2401, 2591 and 3064 are substantive non-answers. Line 227 is internally numerically wrong: recalculating its own sigmoid yields approximately 0.7465 and 0.7585, not 0.71 and 0.82. Line 993 claims a stable system despite 5,000/week demand and 2,000/week capacity. Line 2497 contradicts its own period labels. These failures are established locally; fluent academic prose does not repair them.

Several SFT answers retain useful cores (line 1863 template, line 2711 one-sentence transformation, apology and meeting scripts), but quantitative literature claims, named company cases and institutional/legal detail remain unsupported in the supplied record. Unsupported means not substantiated here, not necessarily proven false. No external factual, legal, medical or financial validation was performed. Film identities, bibliographies, policies and historical assertions should receive specialist verification before use; this audit does not repeat them as facts.

Occam shows strong local instruction alignment: exact entities and Turkish characters, negation, joint conditions, uncertainty, context-sensitive clarification and memory boundaries. It also includes 522-1,407-character explanations/plans, so its signal is appropriate response scale rather than unconditional brevity. Caveats include limited compression, slightly absolute troubleshooting/choir explanations, and context-dependent memory wording. Final-assistant-only supervision is essential: earlier assistant turns in multi-turn examples are context, not targets under the source policy. Historical human-review claims in the card do not change this audit's human_reviewed=false status.

Coverage is observed rather than guaranteed. SFT here is mostly explanatory/advisory, with little evidence for executable coding, strict JSON, actual tool interaction, translation or long-document extraction; its card's capability list does not establish training coverage. Occam covers everyday reading precision and response control, but these 38 rows do not establish advanced mathematics, code execution, broad knowledge or long-context competence. Neither source alone supports a broad Turkish-capability claim. Parent-reported source medians (characters, not tokens): SFT 6,950, Occam 128.5, Atlas 280, CoT 2,719. These are inventory context, not measurements from this purposive review. Parent's SFT replacement-character observation was not independently attributed in this subset; CoT findings are outside scope.

Calibration use: the JSONL supplies evidence-bearing useful/edit/rewrite roles for the parent's planned 15-per-source train-only calibration. These are review suggestions only. For SFT, include the template and short rewrite as useful/edit examples and the three broken targets plus numerical contradictions as negative anchors; do not force 15 positive examples from this bounded subset. For Occam, combine exact extraction, conditions/uncertainty, multi-turn resolution and longer explanation anchors, with the explicitly caveated examples as edit cases. Keep all source eligibility provisional pending provenance/provider-terms review. This content review does not resolve licensing, privacy, contamination, tokenization, truncation or loss-mask implementation.
